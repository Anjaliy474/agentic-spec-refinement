# Clinic Appointment Booking

## Overview
We want a web app that lets patients book appointments at our clinics online. Today patients phone the front desk, which is slow and busy in the mornings.

## Features
- Patients can create an account with their phone number and log in.
- Patients can search for doctors by specialty and clinic location.
- Patients can see a doctor's available slots and book one.
- Patients can cancel or reschedule an appointment up to 24 hours before it starts.
- The app sends a reminder before each appointment.
- Doctors can mark slots as unavailable.
- Front-desk staff can see the day's bookings for their clinic.
- Patients can upload previous prescriptions and lab reports for the doctor to see.

## Notes
The booking page should be fast. Patient data must be kept secure. We expect the app to be popular after launch, especially during flu season.
